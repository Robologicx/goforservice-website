Simple marketplace flow

From service request to completed job

No automatic cheapest-provider selection. Real providers submit offers, and the customer approves the one they prefer.

1

Post your request

Choose a service, describe the work, add photos, location, preferred date and proposed budget.

2

Get eligible offers

Matching checks approved provider status, service, technician skills, coverage and availability.

3

Compare proposals

Review up to 3–5 genuine offers when available, including price, scope, timing and provider details.

4

Approve your choice

You select an offer. The provider confirms availability before the booking is finalized.

5

Track the service

See the assigned technician, use SafeVisit check-in, follow progress and confirm completion.

Trust is part of the product

Verification, assignment control & SafeVisit

Marketplace trust depends on enforcing checks—not simply displaying a “verified” badge.

Separate approvals

Company verification and technician verification are separate. Each technician must have individually approved skills. Companies receive requests only for approved active services.

Customer-approved technician

The customer can see the actual assigned technician. Replacing that technician requires customer approval before the replacement proceeds.

SafeVisit check-in

Booking-specific, expiring visit codes are validated by the backend and tied to the assigned technician. Visit events are recorded for oversight.

Secure platform operations

Role-based permissions, private identity documents, server-side booking and payment controls, audit logs and default-deny access rules are core requirements.
