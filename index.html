A customer-first marketplace

You set the request.
Providers make their offers.

Inspired by open-offer marketplaces, adapted for home services where the job scope and materials matter.

01 · POST

Describe your job

Select a category, explain the work, add photos if helpful, choose a time and enter your expected budget — or ask for quotations.

02 · COMPARE

Receive provider offers

Providers can accept your budget, send a counter-offer or ask questions. Compare price, profile, experience, availability and reviews.

03 · CHOOSE

Hire your preferred provider

Discuss the scope, review the quotation and select the professional you want. The booking records the agreed work and price.

Trust is part of the product

Designed with safer home visits in mind.

GoForService is being designed around clear provider identity, accountable bookings and customer control.

Provider identity review

Planned identity verification and provider profile review before access to eligible customer requests.

SafeVisit arrival OTP

A planned booking-specific code to help confirm the assigned technician at arrival.

Named technician

Customers are intended to see who is assigned to visit, including approved substitutions.

Clear offers

Compare proposed labour, visit fees, materials, exclusions and estimated duration.

Customer-approved changes

Changes to the technician, job scope or price should require customer approval.

Reviews after jobs

Planned reviews linked to completed bookings help customers assess service history.

These are planned capabilities, not claims that every check is already active. Verification labels will state exactly what was checked. No marketplace can guarantee a risk-free visit.
